About Jonathan

Hi! I'm Jonathan — a Grade 11 student at Gloucester High School and Capital Aid's Chief Academic Officer. I'm deeply passionate about building fluency in language, and I aspire to become a Pediatric Bilingual Speech-Language Pathologist. That drive pushes me to help every student find their voice and confidence in communication.

I work with students from Kindergarten through Grade 11, specializing in English language arts and French. Whether it's phonics and reading fundamentals for younger learners, essay writing for middle schoolers, or literary analysis for high schoolers, I tailor every session to meet students exactly where they are.

Outside of tutoring and academics, I play rugby for Gloucester Rugby and love watching TV shows to unwind. I bring that same energy, teamwork, and drive into every tutoring session!
